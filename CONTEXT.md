# Youmotion domain context

## Reminder Assignment

A single scheduled invitation tied to either the Pulse or one positive Leitsatz. It owns enablement, weekdays, times, and, for Leitsatz reminders, its notification-content choice.

## Notification Preview

The per-Leitsatz-reminder choice between a privacy-preserving general message and displaying the positive Leitsatz in the notification. A restrictive Leitsatz is never eligible for notification content.

## Focused Leitsatz Surface

The destination opened from a positive-Leitsatz notification. It presents only the tapped Leitsatz and is the future home for patterns or analytics about that Leitsatz.
