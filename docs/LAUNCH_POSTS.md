## Reddit

Title: I open-sourced my local-first emotion journal built with Expo

I built Youmotion around Pulse, a gesture for recording an emotion's direction, intensity and nuance. You can optionally add a reflection, core belief and guiding belief, then revisit the recorded moments in History and Insights.

It supports iOS and Android, English and German. No account is required; journal entries stay on the device, with user-controlled backup and restore.

Source: https://github.com/thorgas/Youmotion
App and store links: https://youmotion.app

The implementation uses a single XState navigation model, Effect Schema and embedded SurrealDB. I'm interested in feedback on the gesture and local-first architecture. Please use synthetic data in public reports and send sensitive support questions by email.
