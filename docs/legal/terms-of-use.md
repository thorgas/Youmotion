The app allows you to:

- export all local data as a backup file,
- preview and restore a backup (which replaces local data after validation),
- delete all moments and personal beliefs from the device.

You can delete individual entries through the app UI. Exported backups are files you
export; they are outside the app after export and are not deleted by in-app delete
actions.
