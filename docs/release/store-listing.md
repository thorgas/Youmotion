## Full description — English

Youmotion gives you a calm, private way to notice what you feel.

Use the Feeling Pulse to move from a vague sensation to a more precise emotion,
nuance, and intensity. Save a short reflection, optionally connect it with a
personal belief, and return to your history when you want to understand your own
patterns.

Youmotion includes a touch-based seven-direction Feeling Pulse, editable
reflection dates and times, optional personal and supportive belief statements,
local history and insight views, local reminders, English and German support,
and export, restore, and delete controls for local data.

Your journal stays on your device in the current app. You do not need an
account. Youmotion does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care.
