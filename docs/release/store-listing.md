#### Full description — English

Youmotion is a calm, local-first app for private self-reflection.

Start with the seven-direction **Feeling Pulse**. Move from a direction to name a feeling and use the distance to choose intensity. When you finish, your feeling and optional reflection are saved in the local journal.

After the pulse, reflection is brief and optional. You can continue with optional belief work:
core belief (**Leidsatz**) and optional positive guiding statement (**Leitsatz**). No cloud sync is required for any of this.

Saved moments live in **History**. You can filter and search, reopen entries, and inspect **Insights** for your recurring local patterns.

Optional local reminders can invite check-ins at chosen times. All reminders run with your device permissions.

Settings include local backup controls you control: export your full data as JSON, preview and restore a backup, and delete local journal data.

Youmotion has no account and keeps your journal on this device. It supports self-awareness and does not replace psychotherapeutic or medical treatment.

- Confirmed local journal behavior and no account requirement in the app copy and onboarding flow.
