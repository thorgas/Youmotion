# Youmotion app marketing context

## Product

Youmotion is a private, local-first emotion journal for iOS and Android. A check-in begins with a seven-direction emotion star called Pulse. The gesture records a feeling, intensity, and nuance; the user can then add a reflection, an optional core belief, and an optional guiding belief. History and Insights help the user revisit what they recorded over time.

## Launch audience hypothesis

Adults who want a gentle, low-friction way to notice and reflect on emotions without creating an account or turning the experience into a clinical assessment.

This audience is a hypothesis for launch positioning. Validate it with interviews, beta feedback, and store conversion rather than presenting it as established market data.

## Core problem

Many emotion or mood trackers reduce a moment to a label or score. Free-form journals can demand more effort than a person wants to give in the moment. Youmotion sits between them: a quick embodied gesture first, with deeper reflection available but never required.

## Positioning

For people who want to understand emotional moments without judgment, Youmotion is a private emotion journal that combines a nuanced gesture with optional reflection and full-history insights. Unlike account-led trackers or rigid rating scales, entries stay on the user’s device and no feeling is treated as better or worse than another.

## Differentiators

- Pulse captures feeling, intensity, and nuance in one gesture.
- Reflection is progressive and optional rather than a mandatory form.
- Core beliefs and guiding beliefs connect a moment to personal meaning.
- Entries remain local to the device and require no account.
- Insights use the complete recorded history rather than an arbitrary recent-item cap.
- English and German are supported in the app.

## Proof boundaries

Safe claims:

- No account is required.
- User-entered moments are stored locally on the device.
- Users can edit and delete recorded moments.
- Insights are derived from the user’s complete recorded history.
- The app supports English and German.

Do not claim:

- Diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or clinical outcomes.
- Improved mental health, reduced anxiety, or another health result without suitable evidence.
- Anonymous, encrypted, offline-only, or “no data collected” until the release binary and SDK behavior support the exact statement.
- Rankings, awards, user counts, ratings, or testimonials without attributable evidence.
- That one emotion is stronger, healthier, or better than another.

## Voice

Calm, concrete, invitational, and non-judgmental. Prefer “notice,” “hold on to,” “reflect,” and “recorded moment.” Avoid optimization language, pressure, streaks, clinical authority, and exaggerated transformation.

## Launch locales

- `en-US`: original English positioning and keyword research.
- `de-DE`: culturally natural German copy and independent German keyword research.

Localization must preserve the product concepts:

- Pulse: “Pulse” in both locales.
- Core belief: “Leidsatz.”
- Guiding belief: “Leitsatz” or “unterstützender Leitsatz,” depending on context.
- Insights: “Einblicke.”

## Initial category hypothesis

- Apple primary: Health & Fitness
- Apple secondary: Lifestyle
- Google Play: Health & Fitness

Treat these as candidates, not decisions. Compare category fit, competitive density, user expectations, and regulated-medical-device declaration requirements before submission.

## Primary launch goals

1. Pass review with accurate, complete metadata and privacy disclosures.
2. Make the first-three screenshot story understandable without reading the full description.
3. Establish whether privacy-led or reflection-led positioning converts better.
4. Learn which English and German search terms produce relevant impressions.

## Metrics without adding in-app analytics

Use store-provided metrics first:

- Product-page or store-listing visitors
- First-time downloads
- Conversion rate
- Acquisition source and search terms where available
- Crash/ANR and uninstall signals supplied by the stores
- Ratings, review themes, and support topics

Any future in-app analytics or crash SDK is a separate privacy and product decision.
