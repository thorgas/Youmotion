# Public ASO research

Research date: July 24, 2026

## Method and limits

The review used current public App Store and Google Play search results and the launch-stage frameworks described by `Eronred/aso-skills`: marketing context, metadata optimization, competitor analysis, Android ASO, screenshot sequencing, localization, and launch readiness.

The repository says its measured keyword workflow relies on Appeeky for volume, difficulty, rankings, download estimates, and trends. Appeeky was not connected here. Consequently, the recommendations below are relevance-led hypotheses, not measured keyword claims.

## Public listing signals

Relevant listings repeatedly use:

- English: `mood tracker`, `emotion journal`, `feelings tracker`, `self-reflection`, `insights`, `private journal`
- German: `Stimmung`, `Tracker`, `Tagebuch`, `Gefühle`, `Emotionen`, `Achtsamkeit`

Examples:

- Reflect uses “Feelings Tracker” with “Emotion Journal & Insights” and leads with a fast check-in plus patterns.
- Luci positions around “Mood Tracker,” self-reflection, insights, and on-device privacy.
- Feelings Diary uses “Emotion Tracker” and “Private Mental Health Journal,” then emphasizes feelings vocabulary, timeline, analytics, and privacy.

This supports Youmotion's current story—feeling, reflection, patterns—but also shows that generic mood-journal language is crowded. The distinctive proof should stay visible:

1. a continuous emotion star rather than a five-point mood scale;
2. optional core and guiding beliefs;
3. local-only journal content with no account;
4. factual insights over complete recorded history.

## Metadata recommendation

Keep the brand plus a high-relevance descriptor:

- English title: `Youmotion: Discover Feelings`
- English subtitle theme: `Reflect & understand feelings`
- English short-description theme: discovering and understanding feelings
- German title: `Youmotion: Gefühle entdecken`
- German subtitle theme: `Reflektiere & verstehe Gefühle`
- German short-description theme: privates Gefühlstagebuch and Reflexion

The Apple keyword hypotheses were adjusted toward terms visibly used by comparable listings. Do not repeat title/subtitle terms in Apple's keyword field when final measured research can replace them with incremental terms.

## Screenshot recommendation

The strongest first-three narrative is:

1. Name what you feel — the emotion star is the visual differentiator.
2. Hold on to the moment — low-pressure optional reflection.
3. Notice your patterns — visible history and constellation/calendar proof.

The prepared raw screenshots intentionally contain no promotional text overlays. Before upload, the owner can either use the clean UI set or commission localized framed variants following `screenshot-plan.md`. If testing overlays, change one message family at a time.

## Launch decisions still needing measured or owner input

- Validate English and German keyword volume, difficulty, and ranking.
- Confirm category selection: Health & Fitness is the current recommendation; Lifestyle is the alternative to evaluate.
- Complete legal name/trademark clearance because similarly named products and services exist.
- After enough traffic, use App Store Product Page Optimization and Google Play Store Listing Experiments instead of treating the first creative set as final.
