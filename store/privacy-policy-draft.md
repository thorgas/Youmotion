## The short version

Youmotion is designed so that your emotional check-ins, notes, beliefs, history, and insights stay on your device. You do not need an account. We do not use advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following information locally in its app-specific storage:

- emotional check-ins, including the selected feeling, nuance, and intensity;
- optional notes, core beliefs, and guiding beliefs;
- the history and insights calculated from your entries;
- app preferences such as language, emotion-label display, and onboarding status.

This content is not uploaded by Youmotion. You can edit or delete individual entries in the app. Removing the app normally removes its app-specific local data, subject to your device platform's backup and restore behavior.

## Information transmitted for app updates

Youmotion uses Expo Application Services to check for app updates. The update request may include operational information such as the app version, runtime version, platform, and release channel. Your check-ins, notes, and beliefs are not included in update requests.

The update service is provided by Expo / 650 Industries, Inc. Its handling of operational data is governed by its own terms and privacy documentation.

## Information we do not request

Youmotion does not require an account and does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos, health data, or advertising identifier. The current app does not include ads, a marketing analytics SDK, or a crash-reporting SDK.
