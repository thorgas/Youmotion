# Youmotion Support

Youmotion is a private space for noticing feelings, reflecting without pressure, and seeing patterns in the moments you record.

## Contact

Need help, want to report a problem, or have feedback?

Email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** and include:

- your device model;
- your iOS or Android version;
- the Youmotion app version shown in Settings;
- the steps that led to the problem;
- a screenshot, if it does not contain private information.

Do not email private journal entries unless you deliberately choose to share them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Youmotion does not require sign-up or a demo account.

### Where are my check-ins stored?

Your check-ins, notes, beliefs, history, and insights are stored in the app's local storage on your device. The app does not upload that journal content.

### How do I delete a check-in?

Open History, select the moment, and use the delete action. You can also edit a saved moment from its detail screen.

### How do I change the app language?

Open Settings and choose English or German.

### Can I replay the introduction?

Yes. Open Settings and select “Open short guide” / “Kurze Einführung öffnen.”

### Is Youmotion therapy or medical treatment?

No. Youmotion supports self-awareness and reflection. It does not provide diagnosis, psychotherapy, medical advice, or emergency help.

### I am in immediate danger or crisis. What should I do?

Youmotion is not an emergency service. Contact your local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service in your country.
