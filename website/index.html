A simple rhythm

Feel first. Words can follow.

Youmotion does not ask you to explain everything. Start with what is present, then decide how far you want to go.

Private by design

Your journal belongs to you.

There is no account and no cloud journal. Youmotion stores your moments locally and gives you explicit export, restore, and deletion controls.

  • No account
    No registration or social login.
  • No ads or tracking
    No advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.
  • Local reminders
    Optional notifications are scheduled on your device.
  • Your backup
    Export and restore only when you choose.
