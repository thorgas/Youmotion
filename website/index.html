A moment with yourself

Notice what you feel.

A simple rhythm

Feel first. Words can follow.

Youmotion does not ask you to explain everything. Start with the feeling that is present, then choose whether you want to reflect.

1. Find the feeling

Move through the Feeling Pulse. Direction names the feeling; distance captures its intensity.

2. Reflect gently

Add an optional note, notice a core belief, or choose a supportive guiding belief.

3. See your patterns

Return to your local history and insights when you want a wider view.

Private by design

Your journal belongs to you.

There is no account and no cloud journal. Youmotion stores your moments locally and gives you explicit export, restore, and deletion controls.

  • No account
    No registration or social login.
  • No ads or tracking
    No advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.
  • Local reminders
    Optional notifications are scheduled on your device, not sent by remote push.
  • Your backup
    Export and restore only when you choose.
